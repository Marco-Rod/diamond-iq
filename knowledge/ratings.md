# Diamond IQ Player Ratings

Diamond IQ uses four primary hitter ratings:

## Contact

Contact represents a hitter's ability to make effective contact
with the baseball.

The rating is expressed on a scale from 0 to 100.

## Power

Power represents a hitter's ability to produce power when making
contact with the baseball.

The rating is expressed on a scale from 0 to 100.

## Vision

Vision represents a hitter's ability to recognize pitches and
make disciplined hitting decisions.

The rating is expressed on a scale from 0 to 100.

## Overall

Overall is Diamond IQ's aggregate representation of the player's
offensive profile.

The rating is expressed on a scale from 0 to 100.

Ratings must not automatically be interpreted as elite, average,
poor, strong, or weak unless Diamond IQ provides explicit
thresholds or contextual information.
